# Murabaha (Cost-Plus Sale)

## Summary
Murabaha is a sale: a party buys an asset, then sells it to the customer at disclosed cost + markup.

## What to check
- Is there a real asset being purchased and sold?
- Does the financier actually own the asset before selling?
- Is the markup disclosed and fixed?

## Red flags (keywords)
markup, cost-plus, purchase, sell, inventory, commodity, asset

## Missing info questions
- What is the underlying asset?
- When does ownership transfer?
