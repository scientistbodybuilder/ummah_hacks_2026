# Gharar (Excessive Uncertainty)

## Summary
Material uncertainty in the contract terms (price, obligations, penalties, delivery/ownership) is prohibited.

## What to check
- Are key terms “TBD” or at one party’s discretion?
- Are payment terms, penalties, or ownership transfer unclear?
- Are obligations ambiguous?

## Red flags (keywords)
TBD, may change, discretion, subject to approval, unclear, variable later

## What to do
If uncertainty exists, request clarification and recommend scholar review of the clause.
