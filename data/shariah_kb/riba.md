# Riba (Interest) — Core Rule

## Summary
A loan that requires repayment with any guaranteed excess (interest/APR/coupon) is prohibited.

## What to check
- Is this money-for-money with extra repayment?
- Is the return guaranteed regardless of business outcome?
- Are there terms like APR/interest/compounding?

## Red flags (keywords)
interest, APR, rate, compounding, fixed repayment, coupon

## Notes for fintech products
Adding “profit share” does not make a loan-with-interest compliant if the base contract is still a loan.
