# Late Fees / Penalties

## Summary
Penalty clauses require careful handling. A late fee that becomes profit can be problematic.

## What to check
- Does the contract include late fees/penalties?
- Where does the penalty money go (donated vs retained)?
- Does the penalty grow over time (compounding)?

## Red flags (keywords)
late fee, penalty, default fee, missed payment fee, compounding

## What to do
Flag for review and ask: “Are penalties donated (not revenue)?”
