# Musharaka / Mudaraba (Partnership / Profit-Sharing)

## Summary
Profit-sharing is allowed when both parties share risk. Losses typically follow capital contribution.

## What to check
- Is profit share defined as a ratio (not guaranteed amount)?
- Are losses handled fairly (not fully shifted)?
- Is there “guaranteed return” or “capital protection”?

## Red flags (keywords)
guaranteed return, capital protected, principal protected

## Missing info questions
- Are returns guaranteed?
- Who bears losses?
